### Best fit for you: Vanguard FTSE All-World Acc (VWCE)
1. **One fund covers the whole plan:** about 4,000 companies, about 50 countries, developed and emerging markets,
   so there is nothing to rebalance between equity funds for 18 years.
2. **Largest and most liquid** all-world UCITS ETF. Fund closure or merger risk is very low and
   spreads are tight, which matters for a fund you'll hold for decades.
3. **Irish, accumulating:** fits the Luxembourg tax lens (no yearly dividend tax, 15% US withholding at fund level).

**Main trade-off:** if lowest cost matters most, **Amundi Prime All Country World Acc (WEBN)** at 0.07% is a
perfectly reasonable alternative. It is newer and smaller. **SPYI** adds small caps if you want the widest coverage.
Pick one and stick with it. Switching later would mean selling.
