# Luxembourg State Pension: Rough Estimate (2026-11-12)

_Rough estimate in today's euros, not an official calculation. Request the CNAP's own estimate. Not financial or tax advice._

## Assumptions
- Retirement at 65 in 2039, with **25 years** of Luxembourg insurance (12 done + 13 to come)
- Salary stays at **€103,009 gross/yr** in real terms until 65 (below the contribution ceiling)
- Earlier 12 years: three cases (same salary / €85k / €70k in today's money)
- Pension paid in today's money (indexation to prices and the adjustment factor ignored)

## Formula (CNAP)
Monthly pension = **proportional part** + **flat part**
- Proportional: rate × sum of revalued career earnings ÷ 12. Rate is 1.763% for pensions starting in 2026. Under the
  2012 reform it declines towards 1.60% in 2052, so **≈ 1.68% for 2039** (estimate). There is a bonus only if age + years
  exceeds the threshold (95 in 2026): 65 + 25 = 90, so no bonus.
- Flat: €678.71/month for 40 years (from 1 June 2026), pro rata, so 25/40 = **€424**

## Result (gross per month, today's euros)
| Average salary in past 12 years | Proportional | Flat | **Total gross** |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| €103k (same as now) | €3,612 | €424 | **≈ €4,040** |
| €85k | €3,309 | €424 | **≈ €3,730** |
| €70k | €3,057 | €424 | **≈ €3,480** |

Net after health/dependence contributions and tax (class 1): roughly **€2,800–3,200/month** (not computed precisely).
Pensions from **other countries** where the earlier career took place come **on top** (EU coordination).

## Caveats
- The 2025–26 reform (contribution 8.5% each from 2026) may adjust future parameters, and the 2039 rate is extrapolated.
- Salary interruptions, part-time work or career breaks reduce the result.
