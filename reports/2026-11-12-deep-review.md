## 2. Risks of the plan, and how each is handled
| Risk | How big | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| **Market crash** | Global stocks have fallen 40–55% in severe crashes (2000–03, 2008–09) and took years to recover | Long horizon; monthly buying keeps going through crashes; profile says "buy more" |
| **US / Big-Tech concentration** | ~63% US; top 10 companies ≈ 26% of the fund | Accept (market weight), or tilt with an ex-US / EM / equal-weight sleeve (adds complexity) |
| **Currency** | ~75%+ of the assets are in non-EUR currencies (USD mainly) | Diversified across currencies; hedging equity costs money and rarely pays over decades |
| **Valuation** | US valuations are high by history, so future returns may be lower | Plan uses a cautious 4% real return, with a 2–3% scenario shown |
| **Sequence risk** (crash just before or after retiring) | Most damaging in years 15–20 | Glide path to 60–70% equity by 2044, plus ~2 years of spending in safe assets |
| **Inflation** | €2,000 today ≈ €2,860 in 2044 at 2%/yr | All targets modelled in today's money (real returns) |
| **Broker failure** | Securities are customer property, held separately | Keep cash at the broker small; could split across 2 brokers later |
| **Tax-law change** | Luxembourg could change the 6-month rule | Review yearly; nothing to do now |
| **Behaviour** (selling in panic, chasing trends) | Historically the largest cost for private investors | Automatic savings plan, written rules in the profile |

## 3. Where the money really is: profit levers ranked by impact (18 years, 4% real)

| **Execution** (trading in Xetra hours, avoiding wide spreads) | tens of euros per year | Use the savings plan; place the lump-sum order 9:00–17:30 CET |

**Conclusion:** the ETF choice matters far less than (a) the Art. 111bis tax deduction, (b) the monthly
amount, and (c) staying invested. The "most profitable" plan that doesn't add risk is:

1. **€4,500/yr into a low-cost Art. 111bis plan** (if your marginal tax rate is ≥ ~30% and you will be ≥ 60 at payout).
   Look for a plan with **ETF or index funds and total fees ≤ ~1%**.
2. **The remaining ~€525/month (€6,300/yr) + the €20,000 into one global accumulating ETF** at a low-cost broker.
3. **Reinvest the yearly tax refund** (~€1,350–1,750) into the ETF.
