# Retirement Plan: First Projection and Broker Choice (2026-11-12)

_Research only, not personal financial or tax advice. Illustrative projections, not promises._

## Inputs
€20,000 starting capital · €900/month · 18 years (to ~2044) · target €2,000/month extra income (today's money)

## Projection (real returns, i.e. after inflation, in today's euros)
| Real return / yr | Capital at retirement | Perpetual income (3.5%/yr) | Spend capital over 25 yrs (2% real) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 2% | €262,000 | €765/mo | ≈ €1,100/mo |
| 3% | €291,000 | €850/mo | ≈ €1,235/mo |
| 4% (base case for the glide path) | €325,000 | €947/mo | ≈ €1,376/mo |
| 5% | €362,000 | €1,057/mo | ≈ €1,540/mo |

- €2,000/month forever at 3.5% needs about **€686,000** in today's money (about €980k nominal at 2% inflation).
- Base case: about **€950–1,400/month**, so roughly **half to two-thirds of the target**.
- Monthly saving needed for the full €2,000 (perpetual, 4% real): about **€2,000/month**.

## Levers to close the gap
1. Raise contributions with salary (e.g. +3%/yr indexation).
2. Retire 2–4 years later: 20 years ≈ €1,090/mo, 22 years ≈ €1,250/mo (perpetual, 4% real).
3. Spend part of the capital in retirement instead of living on returns only.
4. Art. 111bis private pension plan: €4,500/yr deductible. Compare the tax saving with the product's fees.
5. Clarify the expected Luxembourg state pension. The €2,000 may partly be covered there.
